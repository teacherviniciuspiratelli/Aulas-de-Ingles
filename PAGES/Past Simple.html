📚 Past Simple

Aprenda e pratique o passado simples em inglês

📖 Quando usar o Past Simple

🔹 Usos principais

  • Ações concluídas no passado: I visited my grandmother yesterday.
  • Séries de ações no passado: She woke up, took a shower, and went to work.
  • Hábitos ou rotinas no passado: When I was a child, I played outside every day.
  • Fatos ou estados passados: He was a teacher for 10 years.

🔹 Formação dos verbos regulares

Verbos regulares recebem -ed no passado:

Regra Exemplo
Verbo + -ed walk → walked, play → played
Verbo terminado em -e → + -d like → liked, dance → danced
Verbo terminado em consoante + y → -ied study → studied, cry → cried
Verbo terminado em CVC → dobra consoante + -ed stop → stopped, plan → planned

🔹 Estrutura das frases

Tipo Estrutura Exemplo
Afirmativa Sujeito + verbo no passado She visited London.
Negativa Sujeito + did not + verbo base She did not visit London.
Interrogativa Did + sujeito + verbo base? Did she visit London?
Resposta curta Yes, sujeito + did. / No, sujeito + didn't. Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.

🔹 Expressões de tempo comuns

yesterday last week/month/year in 2010 two days ago when I was a child on Monday in the past
