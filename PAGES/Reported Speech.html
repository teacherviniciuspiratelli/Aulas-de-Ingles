📚 Reported Speech

Aprenda a usar o discurso indireto em inglês

📖 O que é Reported Speech?

🔹 Direct Speech vs Reported Speech

📌 Direct Speech (Discurso Direto)

Repetimos exatamente o que a pessoa disse.

She said: "I am tired." Ela disse: "Estou cansada."
He said: "I will call you." Ele disse: "Vou te ligar."

📌 Reported Speech (Discurso Indireto)

Reportamos o que a pessoa disse sem usar aspas.

She said that she was tired. Ela disse que estava cansada.
He said that he would call me. Ele disse que me ligaria.
↓
Verbo introdutório + (that) + oração
She said (that) she was tired. Ela disse que estava cansada.

* "that" é opcional.

🔹 Mudanças de Tempo Verbal

Quando o verbo introdutório está no passado, os tempos verbais mudam:

Direct Speech → Reported Speech
Present Simple
"I work."		 → Past Simple
He said he worked.
Present Continuous
"I am working."		 → Past Continuous
He said he was working.
Past Simple
"I worked."		 → Past Perfect
He said he had worked.
Present Perfect
"I have worked."		 → Past Perfect
He said he had worked.
Past Perfect
"I had worked."		 → Past Perfect
He said he had worked.
will
"I will work."		 → would
He said he would work.
can
"I can work."		 → could
He said he could work.
must
"I must work."		 → had to
He said he had to work.

🔹 Mudanças de Pronomes e Advérbios

Direct Speech → Reported Speech
I, me, my → he/she, him/her, his/her
we, us, our → they, them, their
you, your → I/me/my ou he/she/him/her
here → there
now → then
today → that day
tomorrow → the next day / the following day
yesterday → the previous day / the day before
this → that
these → those

🔹 Verbos Introdutórios

  • say - dizer (sem objeto)
  • tell - dizer (com objeto: tell someone)
  • ask - perguntar (para perguntas)
  • explain - explicar
  • reply - responder
  • answer - responder
She said (that) she was happy. Ela disse que estava feliz.
She told me (that) she was happy. Ela me disse que estava feliz.

* Use say sem objeto, tell com objeto.

🔹 Perguntas no Reported Speech

Para perguntas, usamos ask e a estrutura de frase afirmativa:

Direct Speech → Reported Speech
Yes/No questions
"Do you like coffee?"		 → if / whether + frase afirmativa
He asked if I liked coffee.
WH-questions
"Where do you live?"		 → WH-word + frase afirmativa
He asked where I lived.
Direct: He said, "Do you like pizza?"
Reported: He asked if I liked pizza.
Direct: She asked, "What time is it?"
Reported: She asked what time it was.

🔹 Ordens e Pedidos

Usamos tell + objeto + to + verbo base.

Direct: He said, "Sit down."
Reported: He told me to sit down.
Direct: She said, "Don't forget."
Reported: She told me not to forget.
