📚 Passive Voice

Aprenda a usar a voz passiva em inglês

📖 O que é a Voz Passiva?

🔹 Quando usar a Voz Passiva

  • Quando o agente não é importante: The car was stolen last night. (Quem roubou não importa)
  • Quando queremos enfatizar a ação: The Mona Lisa was painted by Da Vinci.
  • Em textos formais e científicos: The experiment was conducted in 2020.
  • Quando o agente é desconhecido: My wallet was taken.

🔹 Estrutura da Voz Passiva

Sujeito + to be (no tempo) + Past Participle (V3)
Tempo Verbal Estrutura Passiva Exemplo
Present Simple am/is/are + V3 The house is cleaned every day.
Past Simple was/were + V3 The house was cleaned yesterday.
Future (will) will be + V3 The house will be cleaned tomorrow.
Present Continuous am/is/are being + V3 The house is being cleaned right now.
Past Continuous was/were being + V3 The house was being cleaned when I arrived.
Present Perfect have/has been + V3 The house has been cleaned.
Modal Verbs modal + be + V3 The house must be cleaned.

🔹 Como transformar da Ativa para a Passiva

Ativa: Sujeito + verbo + Objeto

↓

Passiva: Objeto + to be + V3 + (by + Sujeito)

Ativa: John wrote the book.
Passiva: The book was written by John.
Ativa: They are building a new hospital.
Passiva: A new hospital is being built.
Ativa: Someone has stolen my car.
Passiva: My car has been stolen.

🔹 Quando NÃO usar a Voz Passiva

  • Quando o sujeito é importante: ❌ The cake was eaten by me. → ✅ I ate the cake.
  • Com verbos intransitivos (que não têm objeto): ❌ She was arrived. → ✅ She arrived.
  • Em frases informais e conversação: ❌ The party was organized by us. → ✅ We organized the party.

🔹 Estrutura das frases na Passiva

Tipo Estrutura Exemplo
Afirmativa Sujeito + to be + V3 The letter was sent.
Negativa Sujeito + to be + not + V3 The letter was not sent.
Interrogativa To be + sujeito + V3? Was the letter sent?
