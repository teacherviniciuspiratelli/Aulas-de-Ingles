RATHER EM INGLÊS

Guia Completo - Todos os Usos e Estruturas

O que é RATHER?

Rather é uma palavra versátil em inglês que pode expressar preferência, grau, correção e outros significados. É frequentemente confundida pelos estudantes, mas com prática, seu uso se torna natural.

• I would rather stay home. (Prefiro ficar em casa)

• It's rather cold today. (Está um tanto frio hoje)

• Or rather, let me explain better. (Ou melhor, deixe-me explicar melhor)

1. RATHER para PREFERÊNCIA

WOULD RATHER

Expressando preferência entre opções

WOULD RATHER + VERBO (forma base)

• I would rather go to the beach than stay home.

• She would rather study English than math.

• We would rather not wait any longer.

🎯 Forma Negativa

Usamos WOULD RATHER NOT para preferências negativas:

• I would rather not go to the party.

• He would rather not talk about it.

WOULD RATHER + THAT

Preferência sobre ações de outras pessoas

WOULD RATHER + THAT + SUJEITO + VERBO (passado simples)

• I would rather that you came earlier.

• She would rather that he studied more.

• We would rather that they didn't know about this.

⚠️ Atenção ao tempo verbal!

Mesmo falando do presente ou futuro, usamos o passado simples após "that":

• I'd rather you came now. (não "come")

• She'd rather he called tomorrow. (não "calls")

2. RATHER para GRAU/INTENSIDADE

RATHER + ADJETIVO/ADVÉRBIO

Significando "um tanto", "bastante" ou "um pouco"

Neste uso, rather é similar a "quite", "fairly" ou "somewhat".

• The movie was rather interesting.

• She speaks English rather well.

• It's rather difficult to explain.

• He arrived rather late.

🎯 Posição na Frase

Rather geralmente vem antes do adjetivo/advérbio que modifica:

• It's rather cold today. (✓)

• It's cold rather today. (✗)

3. RATHER para CORREÇÃO

OR RATHER

Corrigindo ou especificando algo dito anteriormente

Usado quando queremos corrigir ou ser mais precisos sobre algo que acabamos de dizer.

• I'll see you tomorrow - or rather, on Friday.

• She's a doctor, or rather, a surgeon.

• We need to leave at 5, or rather, 5:15.

• I live in Tokyo, or rather, just outside Tokyo.

4. RATHER THAN - Comparações e Preferências

RATHER THAN

Indicando preferência entre duas opções

• I prefer tea rather than coffee.

• She chose to study rather than go out.

• We decided to walk rather than take the bus.

• He would help rather than criticize.

🎯 Estrutura Paralela

Após "rather than", usamos a mesma forma verbal que vem antes:

• I prefer to read rather than to watch TV.

• She likes swimming rather than running.

5. Usos Formais de RATHER

RATHER + Verbo

Em contextos formais, significa "preferir":

• I rather think you're right.

• We rather suspect something is wrong.

• He rather enjoys classical music.

HAD RATHER

Forma mais formal e menos comum que "would rather":

• I had rather not say.

• We had rather you didn't know.

• She had rather stay home.

Erros Comuns e Dicas

⚠️ RATHER vs. PREFER

Não confunda as estruturas:

• I would rather drink tea. (✓)

• I would rather to drink tea. (✗)

• I prefer to drink tea. (✓)

• I prefer drink tea. (✗)

🎯 Contração Comum

"Would rather" frequentemente é contraído na fala informal:

• I would rather → I'd rather

• She would rather → She'd rather

• We would rather → We'd rather

📚 Resumo das Estruturas do RATHER

Estrutura Significado Exemplo
would rather + verbo Preferência pessoal I'd rather go home.
would rather + that + passado Preferência sobre outros I'd rather that you came.
rather + adjetivo Grau/intensidade It's rather cold.
or rather Correção On Monday, or rather Tuesday.
rather than Comparação Tea rather than coffee.
