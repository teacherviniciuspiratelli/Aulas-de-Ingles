📚 Gerund vs Infinitive

Aprenda a diferença entre Gerúndio e Infinitivo em inglês

📖 O que são Gerund e Infinitive?

🔹 O que é o Gerund?

O gerund é a forma do verbo com -ing que funciona como substantivo.

Verbo + -ing
Swimming is fun.
Nadar é divertido.
I enjoy reading books.
Eu gosto de ler livros.

🔹 O que é o Infinitive?

O infinitivo é a forma do verbo com to + verbo base.

to + verbo base
I want to travel to Japan.
Eu quero viajar para o Japão.
She decided to study medicine.
Ela decidiu estudar medicina.

🔹 Quando usar o Gerund

  • Como sujeito da frase: Running is good for your health.
  • Após preposições: I'm interested in learning Spanish.
  • Após certos verbos (ex: enjoy, avoid, finish): I enjoy traveling.
  • Após expressões como "it's no use", "can't help": I can't help laughing.

Verbos comuns seguidos de Gerund:

admit -ing
avoid -ing
consider -ing
enjoy -ing
finish -ing
imagine -ing
keep -ing
mind -ing
practice -ing
quit -ing
suggest -ing
risk -ing

🔹 Quando usar o Infinitive

  • Para expressar propósito: I went to the store to buy milk.
  • Após certos verbos (ex: want, decide, hope): I want to learn English.
  • Após adjetivos: It's easy to do.
  • Após "too" e "enough": It's too difficult to understand.

Verbos comuns seguidos de Infinitive:

agree to V
decide to V
expect to V
hope to V
learn to V
need to V
offer to V
plan to V

refuse to V
want to V
would like to V

🔹 Verbos que mudam de sentido

Alguns verbos mudam completamente de significado com Gerund ou Infinitive:

Verbo Gerund (-ing) Infinitive (to + V)
remember Lembrar de algo que fez
I remember closing the door.		 Lembrar de fazer algo
I remembered to close the door.
forget Esquecer que fez algo
I'll never forget meeting you.		 Esquecer de fazer algo
I forgot to call you.
stop Parar de fazer algo
I stopped smoking.		 Parar para fazer algo
I stopped to smoke.
try Experimentar fazer algo
Try doing yoga.		 Tentar fazer algo (esforço)
I tried to open it.
I stopped smoking = I no longer smoke.
Eu parei de fumar (não fumo mais).

I stopped to smoke = I stopped what I was doing to have a cigarette.
Eu parei para fumar (interrompi outra atividade).

🔹 Verbos que podem usar ambos sem mudança

begin start continue like love hate prefer
I like swimming / I like to swim.
Eu gosto de nadar. (ambas as formas estão corretas)

📝 Exercício 1: Escolha a forma correta

Escolha se é Gerund (-ing) ou Infinitive (to + V).

1. I enjoy ______ (read) books.
2. She decided ______ (study) abroad.
3. I can't help ______ (laugh) at his jokes.
4. I need ______ (buy) some milk.
5. They kept ______ (talk) during the movie.

✍️ Exercício 2: Complete com a forma correta

Use Gerund ou Infinitive.

6. I want ______ (travel) to Brazil.
7. She avoided ______ (answer) the question.
8. It's important ______ (study) every day.
9. He suggested ______ (go) to the beach.
10. I stopped ______ (smoke) a year ago.

🧠 Exercício 3: Escolha a forma correta (mudança de sentido)

Atenção! A forma muda o significado.

11. I remember ______ (lock) the door. (I did it and I remember)
12. I remembered ______ (lock) the door. (I didn't forget to do it)
13. I stopped ______ (smoke). (I no longer smoke)
14. I stopped ______ (smoke) to take a break. (I paused)
15. I tried ______ (open) the window. (I attempted but it was difficult)
