📚 Present Continuous

Aprenda e pratique o presente contínuo em inglês

📖 Quando usar o Present Continuous

🔹 Usos principais

  • Ações acontecendo agora: I am reading a book right now.
  • Ações temporárias: She is staying with her aunt this week.
  • Planos futuros (já definidos): We are traveling to Paris next month.
  • Mudanças em progresso: The population is growing rapidly.
  • Queixas ou hábitos irritantes (com always): He is always losing his keys.

🔹 Formação do Present Continuous

Sujeito + verbo to be + verbo principal + -ing

Pronome Verbo to be Exemplo
I am I am eating.
You / We / They are You are studying.
He / She / It is She is working.

🔹 Regras para adicionar -ing

Regra Exemplo
Verbo + -ing walk → walking, play → playing
Verbo terminado em -e → tira o -e + -ing write → writing, dance → dancing
Verbo terminado em consoante + vogal + consoante → dobra consoante + -ing run → running, sit → sitting
Verbo terminado em -ie → -y + -ing lie → lying, die → dying
Verbo terminado em -y (sem regra especial) study → studying, cry → crying

🔹 Verbos que NÃO usam Present Continuous

Verbos de estado (stative verbs) geralmente não são usados no contínuo:

  • Pensamento: know, believe, understand, remember, forget
  • Sentimentos: like, love, hate, prefer, want, need
  • Sentidos: see, hear, smell, taste, feel*
  • Posse: have, own, belong, possess
  • Outros: seem, appear, look (no sentido de parecer)
❌ I am knowing the answer.
✅ I know the answer.
❌ She is wanting a new phone.
✅ She wants a new phone.

* "feel" pode ser usado no contínuo quando significa "sentir-se" de forma ativa: I'm feeling better now.

🔹 Estrutura das frases

Tipo Estrutura Exemplo
Afirmativa Sujeito + to be + verbo-ing She is reading a book.
Negativa Sujeito + to be + not + verbo-ing She is not reading a book.
Interrogativa To be + sujeito + verbo-ing? Is she reading a book?
Resposta curta Yes, sujeito + to be. / No, sujeito + to be + not. Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

🔹 Expressões de tempo comuns

now right now at the moment today this week/month/year currently for now
