DO e DOES

Auxiliares para perguntas e negativas no Present Simple

Introdução

Os auxiliares DO e DOES são usados para formar perguntas e frases negativas no Present Simple (tempo presente simples). Eles funcionam como verbos auxiliares que não têm significado próprio, mas são essenciais para a estrutura gramatical.

• Do you like pizza? (Você gosta de pizza?)

• Does she live here? (Ela mora aqui?)

• I don't understand. (Eu não entendo.)

• He doesn't work on weekends. (Ele não trabalha nos fins de semana.)

Quando Usar DO e DOES

DO

Usamos DO com:

  • I
  • You
  • We
  • They
  • Nomes no plural

• Do you like coffee?

• Do they play soccer?

• Do Maria and João study here?

DOES

Usamos DOES com:

  • He
  • She
  • It
  • Nomes no singular

• Does he work here?

• Does she speak English?

• Does your brother live nearby?

Formas do DO/DOES

Forma Afirmativa

NÃO usamos DO/DOES na afirmativa!

I work every day.

She works in an office.

They study English.

Forma Negativa

Sujeito + DO/DOES + NOT + verbo principal

I do not like coffee.

He does not work here.

We do not understand.

Forma Interrogativa

DO/DOES + sujeito + verbo principal

Do you live here?

Does she speak Spanish?

Do they like music?

Contrações

Contrações Negativas

As formas negativas são frequentemente contraídas no inglês falado e escrito:

• do not → don't

• does not → doesn't

• I don't like horror movies.

• She doesn't work on Sundays.

• They don't live here anymore.

Regras Importantes

1. O verbo principal volta para a forma base

Quando usamos DO/DOES, o verbo principal nunca leva -s, -es ou -ies, mesmo com HE, SHE, IT:

• Does she work here? (✓)
Does she works here? (✗)

2. Respostas Curtas

Podemos responder perguntas com DO/DOES usando respostas curtas:

• "Do you like pizza?"
"Yes, I do. / No, I don't."

• "Does he speak English?"
"Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't."

3. Palavras WH- com DO/DOES

Quando usamos palavras interrogativas (what, where, when, why, how), DO/DOES vem depois:

• What do you do? (O que você faz?)

• Where does she live? (Onde ela mora?)

• When do they arrive? (Quando eles chegam?)

Exemplos Completos

Pronome Afirmativa Negativa Pergunta
I I work I don't work Do I work?
You You work You don't work Do you work?
He He works He doesn't work Does he work?
She She works She doesn't work Does she work?
It It works It doesn't work Does it work?
We We work We don't work Do we work?
They They work They don't work Do they work?

Dicas Importantes

🎯 Quando NÃO usar DO/DOES

Não usamos DO/DOES com:

  • Verbo TO BE (am, is, are)
  • Verbos modais (can, could, should, would, etc.)

• Are you happy? (✓)
Do you be happy? (✗)

• Can you swim? (✓)
Do you can swim? (✗)

⚠️ Cuidado com o -S!

Lembre-se: com HE, SHE, IT na afirmativa, o verbo principal leva -s, mas nas perguntas e negativas com DOES, o verbo volta para a forma base:

• She works here. (afirmativa)

• Does she work here? (pergunta - sem -s!)

• She doesn't work here. (negativa - sem -s!)

💡 Pronúncia

• Do → /duː/
• Does → /dʌz/
• Don't → /doʊnt/
• Doesn't → /ˈdʌz.ənt/
